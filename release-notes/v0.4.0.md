New optional sensors and an import action. All new sensors are disabled by default.

- **Streak**: days in a row you logged in Yazio (streak freezes as attribute)
- **Active fasting plan**: e.g. `16:8`, or `inactive`
- **Subscription status** and **subscription end date** (attributes: type, store, start, last change)
- **Action `yazio.import_weight_history`**: copies your Yazio weight entries into Home Assistant's long-term statistics (default: last 5 years)

Streak and subscription are fetched hourly. If they fail, the main sensors keep working.

Requires Home Assistant 2025.6 or newer.
